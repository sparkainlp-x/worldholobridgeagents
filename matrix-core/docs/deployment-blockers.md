# Deployment blockers

This repository is a research prototype. It is not safe or authorized for production installation or payment use until each blocker is independently addressed and documented:

1. Production identity, authentication, authorization, recovery, and trusted actor/tenant context.
2. Durable transactional storage with concurrency, idempotency, audit integrity, backups, retention and deletion controls.
3. Provider capability, signed provider agreements, operational reconciliation and financial/legal review; no execution adapter exists here.
4. Verified, current jurisdiction-specific rules and accountable local review; an unknown jurisdiction must deny.
5. Independent security and privacy threat review, data-protection impact review, incident response and penetration testing.
6. Disability-led accessibility review of software, consent, venue, equipment and stop procedures.
7. Physical-device safety, electrical, environmental and emergency-stop validation by qualified reviewers.
8. Appropriate ethics review, privacy review and accessibility review before any recruitment or human feasibility study.
9. Governance approval, community cultural authority, explicit partner agreement and participant-facing operational materials.

Closing a blocker is not evidence that installation outcomes are validated, funding is confirmed, or any partner is affiliated.
