# Disability-led accessibility review checklist

- [ ] Disabled participants and disability-led reviewers govern priorities, accommodations, stop rules, and interpretation; participation is voluntary and compensated under an approved policy.
- [ ] Review consent and withdrawal flows with screen readers, keyboard-only use, switch/alternative input, captions/transcripts, plain language, translation and offline fallback.
- [ ] Test reduced motion, photosensitivity controls, brightness/viewing distance/duration, audio alternatives, quiet space, sensory load, physical reach and venue access.
- [ ] Offer accommodations without requesting diagnoses; make any accommodation-sharing scope participant-selected and revocable.
- [ ] Test that a participant can stop immediately without finance approval, facilitator mediation, Arena score, or partner referral.
- [ ] Test that shared-installation withdrawal affects only that participant unless the last participant withdraws.
- [ ] Record barriers, failed tasks, burden, assistance and negative findings without identifiable narratives in public reports.
- [ ] Re-review changes to device, installation configuration, venue, language, consent fields, or data recipients.
- [ ] Obtain independent accessibility/privacy/security review before any human feasibility study or deployment.

This checklist is a review aid, not a WCAG or legal conformance certification.
