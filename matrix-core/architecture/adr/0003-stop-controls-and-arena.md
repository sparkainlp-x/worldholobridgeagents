# ADR 0003: Participant stop, withdrawal, and Arena isolation

- **Status:** Accepted for research prototype v0.1.0
- **Decision:** Any participant may immediately stop the shared installation; participant withdrawal is scoped to the participant, and withdrawing the final participant stops the installation. There is no resume transition. Arena fields are optional and have no policy call path.
- **Reason:** Support and safety controls must not depend on financial approval, game participation, or a facilitator's willingness to pause.
- **Trade-off:** Shared installations may pause while a new non-withdrawing participant plan is reviewed; no automatic continuation is inferred.
- **Limit:** This is reference logic, not a physical emergency-stop circuit or a validated human procedure.
