# WorldHoloBridge Conference Demo Concept

## Overview

WorldHoloBridge conference demos present a compact, accessible introduction to community-led holographic AI, inclusive tourism, and evidence-first innovation.

The demonstration uses up to 10 LED hologram fans, adjustable prototype frames, AI persona demonstrations, NFC/QR story access, and optional offline content.

This is a demonstration and pilot concept, not a claim of validated research results.

## Available equipment

- 10 LED hologram fan units
- Adjustable frames and prototype mounting materials, with an allocated build budget of $1,000 USD
- Tablet or laptop control station
- AI persona demonstration
- NFC/QR access points
- Printed accessibility and story cards
- Optional offline content package
