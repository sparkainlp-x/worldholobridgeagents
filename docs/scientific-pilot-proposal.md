# Scientific Pilot Proposal: HoloBridge OS v1.2

## Title

A 90-Day, Community-Led Pilot of a Minimum Viable Country Node for Safe AI Learning, Culturally Co-designed Holographic Personas, and Accessible Phygital Tourism

## Type

Design-framework pilot and feasibility study. This is not a report of validated results.

## Research questions

1. Can a Minimum Viable Country Node operate for 90 days within local community, technical, accessibility, and governance capacity?
2. What accessibility barriers arise in real-world use, and how are they resolved?
3. Can a culturally co-designed AI persona remain within an approved scope using refusal-instead-of-guess and audit-ledger controls?
4. What participation, safety, technical, and governance issues emerge?
5. What should be revised before a larger multi-country rollout?

## Design

Single-site, community-led, pre-registered feasibility pilot with mixed-methods, non-identifying evaluation.

## Participants

Community members, local creators, accessibility testers, cultural-authority representatives, and pilot staff. Participation is voluntary and consent-based.

## Interventions

- Accessible holographic or projection display
- Culturally co-designed AI persona
- NFC/QR learning or tourism trail
- Offline fallback content
- Community storytelling event
- Evidence passport and assurance process

## Outcomes

Primary: completion of a Country Node Evidence Passport.

Secondary: accessibility issues found and resolved; AI persona refusals and escalations; NFC/QR and offline interactions; safety checks, incidents, and near misses; community feedback; governance-firewall compliance.

## Analysis

Descriptive statistics for operational metrics; thematic analysis of qualitative feedback; documented failure capsules. No causal or economic-effect claims will be made.

## Ethics and safety

Informed consent, data minimization, right to withdraw, clear AI disclosure, accessibility accommodations, display and battery safety review, and cultural-authority approval are required.

## Limitations

Small sample, single-site design, no control group, short duration, and context-specific results. Outputs are feasibility findings, not generalizable predictions.

## Deliverables

Evidence passport, failure capsules, accessibility review, safety review, AI persona audit, governance review, lessons-learned report, and continuation recommendation.
