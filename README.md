# WorldHoloBridge

**WorldHoloBridge** is a country-adaptable prototype framework for community-led AI, holographic technology, accessible learning, cultural sovereignty, inclusive tourism, and phygital economic participation.

> **Communities own the stories. Sponsors fund infrastructure. Evidence decides what scales.**

## What WorldHoloBridge does

WorldHoloBridge helps a community, region, or country launch a **Minimum Viable Country Node**: a small, accessible, locally governed pilot that combines:

- Safe AI learning with culturally co-designed persona avatars
- Holographic or projection-based storytelling experiences
- NFC/QR tourism, heritage, and learning trails
- Offline-first access for low-connectivity communities
- Local creator participation and phygital economic models
- Evidence passports, audit ledgers, failure capsules, and reproducibility tools
- A governance firewall separating community authority from sponsor influence

## Status

This repository contains **design frameworks, implementation templates, and research proposals**.

It does **not** claim validated pilot results, confirmed partners, confirmed sponsors, confirmed funding, medical or therapeutic benefits, machine consciousness, or guaranteed NFT value or financial return.

All economic and scenario models are **synthetic illustrations based on labelled assumptions**, not forecasts.

## Minimum Viable Country Node

A Minimum Viable Country Node includes:

- One community partner with cultural authority
- One accessible physical, hybrid, or mobile venue
- One culturally co-designed AI persona
- One NFC/QR tourism or learning trail with 3–5 stops
- One multilingual story collection
- One consent, privacy, and accessibility process
- One evidence passport and failure-capsule process
- One local sponsor or in-kind partner, governed by a firewall
- One pre-registered 90-day pilot protocol

## Assurance principles

WorldHoloBridge uses an evidence-first approach:

- **Evidence passports** document what was tested and learned
- **Persona leases** define approved AI-persona scope and lifespan
- **Audit ledgers** record key decisions, approvals, and incidents
- **Refuse instead of guess** prevents AI personas from inventing sensitive or unverified information
- **Share conclusions, not raw data** protects participant privacy
- **Executable consent gates** make cultural, accessibility, privacy, and payment permissions explicit
- **Failure capsules** make negative results useful and visible
- **Reproducibility passports** make methods and assumptions inspectable
- **No verified lower bound, no headline** prevents unsupported public claims

## 90-day pilot

The first implementation step is a 90-day pilot guided by one question:

> What can we learn from operating one accessible, culturally co-designed HoloBridge Country Node for 90 days?

The pilot produces an evidence passport, failure capsules, accessibility and safety reviews, an AI-persona audit, and a clear recommendation to continue, revise, pause, or close the node.
