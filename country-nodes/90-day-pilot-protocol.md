# HoloBridge Minimum Viable Country Node — 90-Day Pilot Protocol

Version 1.2. Design template and pre-registration-style protocol. Does not claim validated results, partners, sponsors, funding, medical benefits, machine consciousness, or guaranteed economic outcomes.

## Pilot question

What can we learn from operating one accessible, culturally co-designed HoloBridge Country Node for 90 days?

## Phases

- Days 1-14: Preparation
- Days 15-30: Soft launch
- Days 31-75: Public pilot
- Days 76-90: Review and reporting

## Required approvals

- Cultural authority approval
- Accessibility lead approval
- Technical lead approval
- Data-protection approval
- Governance firewall confirmation
- AI persona lease approved
- Display and battery safety review completed
- Evidence passport created

## Primary outcome

A completed Country Node Evidence Passport with participation, accessibility, safety, governance, technical, and lessons-learned findings.

## Stop rules

Pause or stop for cultural-authority withdrawal, serious accessibility/privacy/safety failure, repeated harmful AI output, governance breach, or unresolved hardware risk.

## Public communication rule

No verified lower bound, no headline.
